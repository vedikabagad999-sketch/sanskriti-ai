
Sanskriti AI is an AI-powered cultural guidance platform designed to help people understand and experience the diverse cultural heritage of India.
The platform provides culturally relevant information based on the user's selected destination, including state and city, along with their purpose of travel.

Problem Statement :-
India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage, with every state and region having its own traditions, festivals, customs, food, clothing, languages, etiquette, and social practices.
However, travelers and people relocating to different parts of India often find it difficult to access reliable and relevant cultural information in one place.
Sanskriti AI aims to bridge this gap by providing destination-specific cultural guidance in an accessible and user-friendly way.

 Key Features :-
-  Select an Indian state and destination city
-  Select the purpose of travel
-  AI-assisted cultural guidance
-  State-specific cultural knowledge
-  Information about traditions and festivals
-  Local food and cultural practices
-  Cultural etiquette and do's & don'ts
-  Structured cultural knowledge for different Indian states
-  Simple and responsive web interface
